# The first driven captures: where the band was, what scale the model chose, and what a stopped car measures

Seven capture sessions on 13 September 2026, a phone at the side window of a car at 63–90 km/h
along a dual carriageway, 41 segments measured through the deployed pipeline (`terrain,vegetation`,
`--offset 2`, the grid's defaults). Twelve segments produced no cell at all, another twelve fewer
than ten, and the readings that did come out ran 0.02–0.15 m where the operator expected more.
Everything below was measured on a local bench that re-runs `scripts/assess-grass.mjs` on the
`scene.glb` and `result.npz` the RunPod handler leaves beside each segment's frames, with no GPU
and no change to the depth stage. The bench, its scripts and its logs live outside the
repository in `D:\greenv-runs\`; the packets it produced are reproducible from the same objects.

### The band was on the road side of the track in every usable segment — 2026-09-13

**In 37 of 37 segments with a usable reconstruction, 100% of the back-projected vegetation mask
lay on the negative side of the camera track — the side a positive `offsetM` never reaches.**
`D:\greenv-runs\scale.mjs all`, column "negative side", on every mirrored run; the four
exceptions are the collapsed tracks below, which have no side. On the reference segment
`01a09b0f/1` (run `20260913-140757-87b58d`, 102 frames) the lateral profile of the mask
(`lateral.mjs --every 4`, 889,436 back-projected points) starts at −5 m from the camera, runs to
−9 m at heights of 0.04–0.17 m above the plane, climbs an embankment from −10 to −19 m, and puts
its remaining fifth at −34 to −41 m and 4–12 m up: the tree line. Nothing at all on the positive
side. The shipped band — polyline 2 m to the positive side, 5 m wide, both sides folded — spans
−3 to +7 m and contains only asphalt, which the mask excludes. Retained points per frame: 0 in
all 102 frames, in production and in the local reproduction (`measure.mjs 20260913-140757
--offset 2`: 0 cells of 0, byte-identical outcome).

Flipping the sign alone (`--offset -2`) on all 41 runs: the twelve zero-cell segments became
85–566 measured cells each; over the 37 usable runs the median segment went from 2 measured
cells to 276 (`sweep-left.log`). Distance is as much the problem as side: the near verge sits
5–9 m from the camera behind a shoulder and a guardrail, and no fixed offset knows that.

### DA3's per-clip scale ran from 0.78x to 1.86x against the GPS path of the same frames — 2026-09-13

**The camera track on the fitted plane was shorter or longer than the vehicle's own odometer
by a factor between 0.78 and 1.86 across 37 segments of one drive, median 1.24.** The frame
extractor stamps every sampled frame with `distanceMeters`, its GPS path distance from the
segment's start, so the span between first and last sampled frame is the length the track ought
to have. On `01a09b0f/1` that span is 29.8 m against a 23.3 m reconstructed track: the model's
metres are 22% short, and so is every height measured in them. The per-segment table is in
`scale.log` and the comparison in this session's transcript; the camera's height above the plane,
which a fixed mount keeps constant, scattered from 0.93 to 2.25 m across the same segments and
did not become constant after correction, so it is not the anchor — the track length is.

Correction is one scalar per run, applied to the alignment matrix, the camera centres and the
plane's offset together, and recorded in the packet under `scale` with the model's own numbers
beside the factor. Factors outside 0.5–2 are refused with the blocker `scale-anchor-unusable`:
on this day those were the segments whose track curled (`01a09b11/2`, arc 56.8 m for 14.6 m end
to end) or collapsed, and stretching a wrong shape only makes a bigger wrong shape.

### A camera that did not move measures a door handle at 1.57 m — 2026-09-13

**Four segments reconstructed a camera track of 0.5, 0.7, 1.8 and 6.6 m against 30–170 m
driven, and the pipeline reported them as 1.57, 1.26, 1.42 and 3.93 m of vegetation.** The
contact sheets (`sheet.mjs`) show why: in `01a09b0f/0` and `01a09b10/0` the first 95 frames are
the car's door and dashboard while the phone is being mounted; in `01a09b15/0` the car is stopped
at a painted chevron and the only vegetation is a tree; `01a09b19/0` likewise. With the whole
cloud a metre across, whatever the mask touched — a door handle, a mirror, a tree — filled the
ten cells of a 0.5 m grid and became the segment's tallest reading. The gate `minTrackM` refuses
a track shorter than the caller's floor with `camera-track-too-short` and no cells; the
deployment sets 3 m.

### What the automatic band, the anchor and the gate give together

`scripts/grass-anchor.mjs` (pure, tested) and three request fields in `runGrassPipeline`:
`offsetSide: "auto"` places the road edge at the 10th percentile of the mask's lateral distance
less 0.5 m, on the side that holds at least 70% of it, and widens the band to the 90th percentile
within 5–10 m; `trackLengthM` anchors the scale; `minTrackM` gates the track. Defaults leave
every one of them off, so a walked fixture measures exactly as recorded. On the reference
segment the three together give 659 measured cells of 833, band at −7.2 m and 10 m wide, scale
×1.28, `extent95M` p50 0.16 m and p95 0.33 m, and the near verge's cells (0–3 m from the edge)
read 0.12–0.22 m with 19–24 frames each while the embankment's own ground climbs from 0.42 to
1.10 m and its extents stay at 0.09–0.19 m — the per-cell datum following the slope as designed.
`check-grass-quality.mjs` passes on the packet. The 41-run sweep with all three on is in the
section below.

### The 41-run sweep with band, anchor and gate on — 2026-09-13

`D:\greenv-runs\measure.mjs all --vehicle` (`sweep-vehicle.log`, 43 runs including two
measured after the day's first pass), each run's `trackLengthM` taken from its own manifest:

| | Production packets | Re-measured with the three options |
|---|---:|---:|
| Segments with no measured cell | 20 of 43 | 4 (three refused as `camera-track-too-short`, one with no ground plane) |
| Measured cells, all segments | 900 | 24,603 |
| Median measured cells per usable segment | 2 | 614 |
| Scale factor applied | — | 34 runs, 0.78x–1.86x; 7 refused out of range (curled or collapsed tracks) and measured at the model's scale, 2 not reached |
| Band placed from the masks | — | 40 runs, all on the negative side, edge 1.4–8.9 m from the camera, width 10 m in all but one |
| `extent95M` p50 of the cells, across usable segments | 0.00–0.40 m on 1–244 cells | 0.06–0.42 m (one stopped-car outlier at 1.38 m) |

The zero-cell segments were the band's doing and nothing else: every one of them measures
263–844 cells once the band is where the mask is.

**What the sweep also shows: the far end of a 10 m band reaches the tree line.** In 9 of the
40 banded runs, 10–25% of the measured cells read more than 2 m of extent, all of them 5–10 m
from the edge with a local ground 0.5–5.4 m above the road — trees, walls and cut faces the
`vegetation` class claims and the per-cell datum cannot undo (`compare.py`, 7e/2: 155 of 757
cells above 1 m, at 6.2–9.8 m from the edge). They do not move the dashboard's level, which is
already 3 above 0.30 m, but they put the segment's p95 at 3.85–8.65 m where the mowable verge
reads 0.5–1.4 m. Excluding cells above 2 m from the aggregate leaves every level unchanged
across the 43 segments and brings those p95 values to 0.52–1.85 m; which aggregate a mowing
policy should use is the open question in `GRASS-QUALITY.md`, not a parameter here.

### Two ceilings take the trees out of the aggregate and nothing else — 2026-09-13

`--vehicle` plus `gridOptions: { maxHeightM: 3, canopyExtentM: 2 }` on the same 43 runs
(`sweep-canopy.log`, `compare.py`): a back-projected point higher than 3 m above the road plane
never enters a cell, and a cell whose extent above its own ground still exceeds 2 m is reported
as `canopy` and counted in no aggregate.

| Segment | p95 with band and anchor | p95 with the ceilings | cells set aside as canopy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 7e/2 | 8.65 m | 0.63 m | 30 |
| 7e/3 | 5.16 m | 1.58 m | 23 |
| 19/4 | 4.83 m | 0.39 m | 0 (the crowns were above 3 m; 152 cells lost their points) |
| 7e/0 | 4.57 m | 0.56 m | 0 |
| 19/5 | 3.85 m | 1.13 m | 5 |
| 19/2 | 1.68 m | 0.97 m | 13 |
| 19/3 | 1.31 m | 0.66 m | 9 |

Across the 43 runs the ceilings set 87 cells aside as canopy and removed the points of 1,395
more, 24,603 measured cells to 23,121; 31 of the 39 banded runs did not change by a single cell.
No dashboard level moved — everything above 0.30 m was level 3 before and after — and the p50 of
every run moved by at most 0.09 m. The residue is honest: 7e/3 still reads a p95 of 1.58 m and
the stopped car of 15/0 1.45 m, because low branches and a hedge at 1.5–2 m are, by these two
numbers, vegetation a crew would meet. Where the verge ends and the hedge begins is a policy
line, and 2 m is the draft.

### A wet road hides the floor, and a coarser fit finds it — 2026-09-13

**Segment `01a09b11/13` (run `20260913-150211-232fa1`) measured nothing because no ground plane
was found: the best hypothesis held 0.80% of the cloud against the 1% floor.** Rain, and a road
that reflects the sky; the depth model reads the reflection as depth scattered below the
surface, so along gravity the cloud's 1st percentile sits 0.90 m under its median where the
healthy run `0f/1` has 0.47 m (`floor.mjs`). The true ground is a thin layer no 3.5 cm plane
can gather 1% of. Halving the support floor recovers a plane at 5.4° with 0.8% inliers; twice
the inlier distance (0.07 m) gives 1.6% at 5.7°, RMSE 0.041 m, 1.4% of the cloud below it, and
the same track length as every other tolerance tried (29.1 m). The camera stands 2.2 m above
each of them, which is a height a car camera can have and a wall or a reflection cannot.

`groundFallback: true` makes that one coarser attempt when the strict fit fails, keeps it only
if the camera stands 0.3–6 m above the result, and records `ground.relaxedFit` and the blocker
`ground-fit-relaxed`. With it and the vehicle options the segment measures 601 cells of 815:
`extent95M` p50 0.28 m, p95 0.57 m, band 5.9 m out on the negative side, scale ×1.37. Off by
default; the deployment turns it on.

### The frames float, a crown floats, and the corridor is five metres — 2026-09-14

**A mown verge the photographs put at 10–15 cm read 16–28 cm because the frames of a driven
capture do not agree about where the ground is.** On `0f/1`, `11/13` and `7e/1` a cell's
heights spread 1–8 cm *within* one frame (per-frame `h95 − h50`) and 24–52 cm *between* frames
(`h95SpreadM`); `7e/1`, where the float was 5–11 cm, was the one verge that read 4–8 cm. The
pooled datum takes the 2nd percentile of every voting frame together — the lowest floating
frame — while the canopy percentile is the median frame's, so half the float became grass.
`datum: "per-frame"` measures each frame's percentiles against that frame's own 2nd percentile
and takes the median across frames: `0f/1` p50 0.16 → 0.09 m, `11/13` 0.28 → 0.11, `7e/1` 0.08
→ 0.04. `geometry/grass-height-grid.test.ts` pins it on five frames of one lawn, three of them
floated 0.30 m: pooled reads over 0.30 m of grass, per frame reads the lawn.

**A branch at two metres passes under a 3 m point ceiling and inside a 2 m extent.** `7e/3`
read a p95 of 1.58 m from the tree beside the road. What separates a crown from a hedge or tall
grass is not height but the air under it: a frame sees the ground, then nothing for half a
metre or more, then foliage, where a plant is continuous from the ground up. `canopyGapM: 0.5`
— the widest gap between consecutive voxel heights with a tenth of the voxels above it, median
across frames — sets 27 cells of `7e/3` aside as `floating-above-ground` and its p95 falls to
0.99 m with the 10 m band, 0.30 m with the corridor below. No cell of `0f/1` is touched.

**The band followed the vegetation out to 10 m and climbed the slope behind the corridor.**
`19/5` measured its mown strip and the brush above it together, p95 1.13 m. Five metres from
the road edge is what a crew cuts; `maxDistanceFromRoadM: 5.5` from the auto-placed edge, which
sits half a metre before the vegetation starts, holds the band to that: `19/5` 1,804 → 1,489
cells, p95 0.82 m.

The three together on the 43 runs (`sweep-final.log`, `compare.py --tags canopy,final`): 40
measured, 17,699 cells, 26 set aside as canopy; p50 2–18 cm on 37 of the 40, 27 cm on `19/2`
and 41 cm on the stopped car of `15/0`; p95 6–92 cm; dashboard levels 7 × 1, 11 × 2, 22 × 3
where the previous round had 34 × 3. The p95 that decides the level is the top twentieth of the
cells: a hedge or a touceira at the corridor's edge is enough to hold a mown strip at level 3,
and whether the level should follow p50, p95 or the share of cells above the threshold is the
policy question `GRASS-QUALITY.md` leaves to Motiva.

**Scale, checked against the guardrail.** In `0f/1` the cloud's height profile in the lateral
strip [−3.6, −1.8] m from the camera ends sharply at 0.51–0.52 m above the plane: the top of the
W-beam, which the standard puts at 0.70–0.75 m. That asks for ×1.35–1.45; the GPS anchor gave
×1.28. The two agree, and neither is what inflated the verge. `19/5`'s ×1.66 puts its vegetation
10 m from a camera whose photograph shows a narrow shoulder, and stays an open question.

### The rail's lower edge, and the wet road on the wrong side of the line — 2026-09-14

**On `11/7` the twelve tallest cells, 40–57 cm on a strip that reads 3–9 cm, are the last half
metre against the guardrail.** Their own pixels painted back on frame 74 (`cellpix.mjs`) sit
along the rail's lower edge: at 128×128 logits a class pixel is 4.5 by 8 photograph pixels, and
the `terrain` beside a `fence` carries the rail with it. `excludeNearClasses:
"fence,wall,pole,building"` with a one-pixel radius drops 15,922 of 278,345 mask pixels on that
run and takes the segment's p95 from 31 to 29 cm; a three-pixel radius would take it to 24 cm
but starts on vegetation beside any wall, so one it is. What stays above 30 cm there is the
metre of grass a mower does not reach under the rail — 15–45 cm, and real.

**In rain the road is `vegetation`.** The class map of frame 9 (`mask.mjs`) gives the wet
asphalt to `vegetation` for a fifth of the frame: the reflection of the trees. The band folded
both sides of the edge together, so the road side entered as cells of 0 cm. `bandSide`, set by
the auto-placed edge to the vegetation's side, keeps only that side; 17,699 cells become
17,060 across the 43 runs, p50 moves by at most 1 cm on 40 of them, and four segments whose p95
sat just past 30 cm from those rows step down to level 2 (`0f/1` 39 → 29, `10/1` 41 → 15,
`11/2` 32 → 30, `11/7` 31 → 29). Levels 6 × 1, 14 × 2, 20 × 3.

### Not established here

No reading on this page has been compared with a tape; the day produced no reference
measurement. The band's far edge is a percentile of a mask that includes tree lines, so the 10 m
cap is doing real work and has not been graded. The scale anchor trusts the phone's GPS odometer
over 30–40 m with 2–5 fixes per segment; the extractor's `distanceMeters` is its own
interpolation of those fixes, and its error has not been measured. And the measurement still
covers the first 30–40 m of each 170–250 m segment (`groups[].published` in the manifest), which
is a coverage decision outside this pipeline.
